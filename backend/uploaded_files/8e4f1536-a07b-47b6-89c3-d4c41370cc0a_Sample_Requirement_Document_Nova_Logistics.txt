PROJECT REQUIREMENT DOCUMENT
Client: Nova Logistics Pvt. Ltd.
Project: Warehouse Inventory Management System

1. OVERVIEW
Nova Logistics wants a web-based inventory management system to replace
their current Excel-based tracking. The system should help warehouse staff
track stock levels, manage suppliers, and alert managers when items run
low.

2. USER MANAGEMENT
The system should support three types of users: Warehouse Staff, Managers,
and Admins. Admins can create and remove user accounts. Managers can view
reports and approve purchase orders. Warehouse staff can only update stock
counts and mark items as received or shipped.

3. INVENTORY TRACKING
Every item in the warehouse needs a unique SKU code, a name, category,
current quantity, minimum threshold, and unit price. Staff should be able
to search for items by name or SKU. When an item is received or shipped,
the quantity should update in real time.

4. LOW STOCK ALERTS
When an item's quantity falls below its minimum threshold, the system
should automatically notify the assigned manager by email. The dashboard
should also show a list of all low-stock items so managers can see them
at a glance without waiting for an email.

5. SUPPLIER MANAGEMENT
Admins need to maintain a list of suppliers with contact details, the
items each supplier provides, and average delivery time. When creating a
purchase order, the system should suggest the best supplier based on past
delivery performance.

6. PURCHASE ORDERS
Managers should be able to create a purchase order for one or more items,
send it to the selected supplier, and track its status (pending, shipped,
received). Once a purchase order is marked received, the corresponding
inventory quantities should update automatically.

7. REPORTING
The system should generate a weekly report showing: total stock value,
items sold or shipped that week, items received that week, and a list of
items currently below their minimum threshold. Reports should be
downloadable as PDF or Excel.

8. NOTIFICATIONS
Beyond low-stock alerts, the system should notify managers when a
purchase order is overdue (past its expected delivery date) and when a
new user account is created, for approval.

9. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
The system should be accessible on both desktop and tablet, since
warehouse staff often use tablets on the floor. Data should be backed up
daily. The system should support at least 20 concurrent users without
noticeable slowdown.

10. TIMELINE
Nova Logistics would like a working version covering inventory tracking
and low-stock alerts within 6 weeks, with supplier management and
purchase orders following in a second phase.
